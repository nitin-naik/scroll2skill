# Friction and coverage gaps: short-form-edit-plan on task_edit

## 1. Friction

1. **Music drop vs. adding music in the app.** Steps 4 and 8 contradict each other:
   - Step 4 lists "a sound or music drop" as an interrupt and says rhythm includes "steady music".
   - Step 8 says "export with voice and sound effects only and add music inside each app".
   - If the music is added in each app, the editor can't time a music drop, and can't place the music for rhythm either.
   - **Guess:** I didn't use a music drop as an interrupt. I used a punch-in and a full-screen card instead.

2. **"Pick one [pace] and keep it consistent" vs. "Plan b-roll for every concrete noun".** Steps 5 and 6 pull against each other:
   - Step 5's "One-take with overlays: no cuts at all".
   - Step 6's "Plan b-roll for every concrete noun… keep each 1–3 s".
   - Cutaway b-roll is a cut, so it conflicts with the one-take pace.
   - **Guess:** I treated all b-roll as overlays or picture-in-picture, which step 4 counts as rhythm.

3. **"One-take with overlays: no cuts at all" vs. step 1.** Step 1 says to remove gaps with jump cuts "for both pace styles". It's clear that jump cuts are allowed, but "no cuts at all" in step 5 still reads as a contradiction.
   - **Guess:** "cuts" in step 5 means scene or shot changes.

4. **"around the halfway point".** It's unclear whether "halfway" means half of the original running time or half of the trimmed one. The tip structure made exactly 20 s an awkward spot.
   - **Guess:** I put interrupt 2 at about 25 s, on "on mute", which is a little past half of the trimmed 40 s. I chose that because the content suited the card.

5. **"The first frame must already show the payoff, a number or a person mid-gesture".**
   - It's unclear whether this means one of these or all of them.
   - It's also unclear whether reordering spoken lines is allowed when it breaks the logic of the talk. "this phone" is said to the phone that's filming it.
   - **Guess:** any one of them is enough, but I combined all three. I reordered the 50K line to 0:00.

6. **"Put 2–4 words of hook text on screen by 0:00.5".** The skill doesn't say how long the hook text stays, where it sits relative to the captions, or whether it can repeat the first caption.
   - **Guess:** top-centre, held for about 2 s.

7. **"Show structure… a segmented progress bar… that fills as you go".** The skill gives no CapCut method for building it.
   - **Guess:** text or shape stickers *(inferred)*.

8. **"Hold the final text card long enough to read (≈1.5 s)" vs. "End on the CTA, then cut… No outro".** It's unclear whether a card held after the CTA counts as an outro.
   - **Guess:** a 1.5 s card is allowed. "see you there" was deleted.

9. **"1 emphasis word per line" with "numbers and the key noun".** "Line" could mean a caption chunk or a spoken line, and the rule allows one word but names two kinds.
   - **Guess:** one per spoken line (one per table row). Some rows still feel like they want two.

10. **"Times after trimming are estimates; say so".** The skill gives no method for estimating trimmed durations, such as words per second or a per-cut saving.
    - **Guess:** about 0.3 s saved per removed gap or filler.

11. **Output 5's "upload-quality settings".** The skill names it but gives no values.
    - **Guess:** 1080×1920, 30 fps or matching the source, and Instagram's "Upload at highest quality" *(inferred)*.

12. **Output 4's "Premiere: Transcribe" vs. CapCut.** Only CapCut's auto-captions menu is given. Menus for single-word colour, keyframe-free scale, overlays and masks all had to be inferred.

## 2. Coverage gaps

- **No rule for reordering spoken lines** when the payoff is buried at 0:04. The skill doesn't cover how to keep the logic intact, as with "this phone" when you move a line.
- **No guidance on the hook text's wording vs. the spoken hook**, for example whether they should match or complement each other.
- **No tie-in between the CTA and part 2.** The skill doesn't say whether to pin a comment or add a "Part 2" playlist or series link. For Shorts, the related-video link is a real feature *(not in skill)*.
- **No caption or title copy rules.** Output 5 asks for a Reels caption and a Shorts title but gives no length or hashtag guidance.
- **No numeric music level for in-app mixing.** It says "low volume" but gives no number.
- **No safe-zone numbers for the top of the frame**, beyond the inferred 12% and 20%.
- **Nothing on whether to post the same file to both platforms or make platform-specific variants**, or on posting order and timing.
- **No guidance on a part 1 / part 2 series**, which the user's CTA sets up.
- **No guidance on filming the extra b-roll the plan asks for**, such as framing, keeping the same light and phone settings, or vertical format.
