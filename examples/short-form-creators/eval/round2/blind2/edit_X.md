## 2. First frame (0:00, the most important frame)

- **Picture:** you holding the phone up toward the lens. If you didn't hold it in the take, film a 1-second insert of the phone in your hand and put it under the opening words.
- **Text, top third, large, two lines:**
  > **0 → 50K subscribers**
  > **shot on THIS phone 📱**
- **At "fifty thousand subscribers" (about 0:03):** flash a YouTube Studio screenshot of your subscriber count for about 1 second, scaled in from 90% to 100%. This proof is what keeps viewers from swiping.
- Use the same frame as your **Reels cover** and **Shorts thumbnail**. On Reels, check the 3:4 grid crop: keep the text in the centre band.

## 4. Captions

- Use CapCut **Auto captions**, then fix them by hand: write "50,000" or "50K", not "fifty thousand", and remove the "okay so" / "um" if any remain.
- Use **2–4 words per line**: bold sans font, white text with a black stroke or shadow.
- **Highlight keywords** in one accent colour (yellow works): *this phone, 50K, first three seconds, swipe, payoff, pause, mute, window.*
- **Position:** just below your chin, around 55–65% down the frame. Keep everything out of the **bottom ~20%** and the **right ~12%** (likes/comments/share buttons on both apps), and out of the top ~10%.
- Don't let the tip labels and captions overlap. Labels go at the top third, captions at the lower-middle.

---

## 5. Audio and music

- Voice first: **Enhance voice / Reduce noise** on, and normalise loudness so all clips match.
- Music: an upbeat, light instrumental (lo-fi or soft electronic), **very low under your voice**, around 10–15% volume in CapCut, roughly −25 to −30 dB under the dialogue. If you have to strain to hear it, it's right.
- **Better option:** export a version **without music** and add a trending or library track inside Instagram and YouTube at upload, set very low. Platform-library audio avoids copyright mutes, and on Reels it can help discovery. CapCut tracks marked "commercial use not allowed" can get muted.
- SFX: just 3–4 in total (pop on the screenshot, soft whoosh on the swipe b-roll, tick on each tip label). More than that sounds cheap.

---

## 6. Ending and loop

- End right after the CTA. **Don't add an outro card**, and **delete CapCut's auto-added ending clip / watermark** if your version adds one.
- Optional loop trick: finish on "that's literally all I did" with a hard cut, so it flows straight back into "I started with this phone". It's good for replays, but you'd lose the Part 2 CTA, so use it for the Shorts version if you want.

## 7. Platform versions (export twice)

**Instagram Reels**
- Keep "follow for part two".
- Caption: *"0 → 50K on a phone. The 4 things that actually mattered 👇 Part 2 (my exact CapCut setup) drops [day]."*
- Turn on *Upload at highest quality* in the Instagram settings.

**YouTube Shorts**
- YouTube's word is "subscribe", not "follow". Either re-record one line ("subscribe for part two, where I show my exact editing setup"), or keep your audio and change the on-screen text to **"Subscribe for Part 2 →"**.
- Once Part 2 is live, attach it using Shorts' **Related video** link. That puts a tappable link right on the Short.
- Title: *"I got 50K subscribers with just my phone (4 rules)"*.

**Export (both):** 1080×1920, same frame rate you shot at (usually 30fps), highest bitrate CapCut offers, 9:16.
