# Friction and coverage gaps: short-form-script skill (v2), SIP reel task

## 1. Friction

- "offer both if unsure" (step 0): unclear whether "both" means two full scripts or just the spoken lines. Guessed: a full English table plus a Hinglish version of the spoken column only, with on-screen text shared.
- "Spoken hook ≤ 8 words ... containing a specific number or a concrete noun": unclear whether "₹10 lakh" counts as one word or two, and whether symbols count. Guessed: counted it as two words, so the hook is 8.
- "never speed up the delivery" + "At about 2.5 spoken words per second": unclear whether the 30–40 s target includes a silent disclaimer card or end hold. Guessed: spoken audio ≈ 34–38 s, with a silent 2 s disclaimer card to reach 40 s.
- "with 3 points, shorten the lead to 2–4 s so each point gets ~7 s": the arithmetic only works if the open-loop lines are counted inside the ~7 s. 3 s hook + 3 s lead + 3×7 s + 2 open loops (~2 s each) + CTA (~4 s) + disclaimer caveat is about 35–40 s. Unclear which. Guessed: open loops are separate 2 s beats, and point 3 runs longer (10 s) because it carries the number.
- "the frame from step 2 goes into the hook or the lead": unclear what to do when the user has no personal result, since step 2's preferred frame is personal and the skill forbids inventing one. Guessed: number-first frame in the hook, plus an optional swap line if the user has a real story.
- "Body beats: 2–5 words of on-screen text each": the beat that carries the example number needs a footnote ("example: 12% vs 11% p.a., 20 yrs") that breaks the limit. Guessed: kept the main text short and allowed a small footnote, because step 7 requires return figures to be labelled as assumptions.
- "A spoken call to action must never push an irreversible or taxable action ... without the caveat in the same breath": the switch suggestion here is inside point 3, not the CTA. Unclear whether the rule covers that. Guessed yes, and put the exit-load/tax line right after it.
- "Titles/captions: 2 options ... For Instagram: a first line that repeats the hook, 1–3 lines of value, any required disclaimer, 3–5 specific hashtags" and output item 5, "Ready-to-paste caption": these overlap. Unclear whether item 3 is title lines and item 5 the full caption. Guessed: item 3 is two short first-line/title options, and item 5 is one full caption.
- "plus how many takes per line" (filming notes): no number is given. Guessed: 3 for the hook, 2 for other lines.
- "Mark anything that came from your own knowledge rather than the sources as *(inferred)*" vs step 7's "*(own knowledge, verify current rules)*": two different marker labels. Guessed: used the step 7 label for regulatory items and *(inferred)* for the rest.
- "Answer the user's actual request first ... finished script comes first" vs Output order, which puts the payoff/frame before the table: unclear whether a short payoff/frame preface is allowed before the script. Guessed: kept a 3-line preface, then the script.

## 2. Coverage gaps

- **Topic substance:** the skill has no guidance on choosing the actual content (which three mistakes, and whether they're true and non-advisory). The whole quality of this reel depends on that, and I had to supply it from my own knowledge.
- **How to compute and present an example figure:** the skill doesn't cover monthly-compounding conventions (r/12 vs effective annual), or whether to pick the conservative figure. Online calculators give ~₹12.6 lakh and the stricter convention gives ~₹10.4 lakh. A complete answer needs to pick one and say why.
- **Personal-finance specifics:** the exact wording of the standard Indian MF disclaimer, how long it must stay on screen, and whether it must be spoken. The skill only says "add what the topic needs".
- **Pronunciation / gender variants for Hinglish** ("karta/karti hoon"): not mentioned.
- **Captions/subtitles burned into the video** (as an editing step): only implied by "most viewers start muted". Not asked for in Filming notes.
- **Cover/thumbnail frame for the Instagram grid:** not asked for. It matters for a profile with 8k followers.
- **Audio/music choice** (trending audio vs voice only): explicitly out of scope, but a "ready to film today" Reel usually needs a decision.
- **Instagram-specific length and pacing:** the sources are mostly about YouTube Shorts, so Instagram Reel norms (e.g. on-screen safe zones away from the caption/UI overlay at the bottom and right) aren't covered.
