# Edit plan: "You don't need a fancy camera" (Reels + Shorts, CapCut)

**What changes:** the 52 s take becomes a **~40 s** cut. The "50K subscribers" line moves to the start, every pause and filler goes, "see you there" is cut, and the video ends on the part-two CTA.
**Pace:** cut-driven. Removing the pauses leaves about 12 jump cuts in 40 s, roughly 3 per 10 s. Hide each jump cut with a small scale change (alternate 100% and 108%) so it reads as rhythm. Section labels at the top show the 4-tip structure.

## 4. App steps (CapCut)

Menu names change between CapCut versions and between mobile and desktop, so check the names in your version.

1. **Reorder:** split the clip at 0:04 and 0:09 (move the playhead, then **Split**). Drag the 0:04–0:09 piece to the start of the timeline.
2. **Cut pauses and fillers:** for each gap and each "okay so", "so", "and um yeah" and "see you there", move the playhead to its start, **Split**, move to its end, **Split**, then select the piece and **Delete**. CapCut may have an automatic option such as **Remove silences** or **Smart cut** *(inferred)*. If you use it, still check the "um" by hand. Silence detection keeps filler words because they aren't silent.
3. **Hide jump cuts:** select every other clip, go to **Basic → Scale** and set 108%. Leave the clips in between at 100%.
4. **Punch-in at 0:08 (interrupt 1):** select that clip and add two **Scale keyframes**, 100% at the start and 130% about 0.15 s later. Hold 130% until 0:09.5 *(inferred)*.
5. **Captions:** **Text → Auto captions**, language English. Then fix every chunk by hand:
   - "fifty thousand" → **50K**, "eight months" → **8 MONTHS**, "three seconds" → **3 SECONDS**, "part two" → **PART 2**
   - Split long captions into chunks of 1–4 words
   - Look for misheard words near "YouTube" and "subscribers"
6. **Caption style:** select a caption, go to **Style**, and set:
   - Bold sans-serif font, white, all caps, black outline or shadow
   - Applied to all captions *(inferred: the "apply to all" toggle)*
   - Then select each emphasis word and set it yellow at about 115% size
   - Some versions have a **highlight keywords** option in the caption templates *(inferred)*. Check that it picks the words from the table, not random ones.
7. **Placement:** put the captions at centre or slightly below. Keep them out of the bottom ~20% and the right ~15% of the frame, where the like, comment and share buttons and the post caption sit *(inferred)*.
8. **Section labels and hook text:** **Text → Add text** for "TIP 1/4" … "TIP 4/4", the hook line and the CTA card. Set each one's duration on the timeline to match the edit table.
9. **B-roll:** record your screen while you cut a pause in CapCut. Add the recording with **Overlay → Add overlay** and stretch it to fill the frame from 0:21.0 to 0:23.0.
10. **Sound:**
    - Music: **Audio → Sounds**. Set it to about 15–25% volume under your voice *(inferred)*, and cut it to silence from 0:21.0 to 0:23.0.
    - Whoosh and pop: **Audio → Sound effects**, then search "whoosh" and "pop".
11. **Export:** 1080×1920, 30 fps.

## 5. Platform notes (Reels vs Shorts)

- **Length:** ~40 s works on both. No changes needed.
- **CTA wording:** "follow" is Instagram's word. For Shorts, export a second version with the card changed to **"SUBSCRIBE FOR PART 2"**. You can leave the spoken "follow" alone. When part 2 is up, link it from the Short as a related video *(inferred)*.
- **Music:** some CapCut tracks aren't cleared for every platform *(inferred)*. The safest option is to export with your voice and sound effects only, then add music in each app's own library at low volume (Instagram audio picker, YouTube Shorts "Add sound").
- **Cover:**
  - Reels: pick the first frame, or add a cover that repeats "50K SUBS. ONE PHONE." This cover shows on your profile grid.
  - Shorts: choose the cover frame in the YouTube mobile app when you upload. Pick the same frame *(inferred)*.
- **Safe zones:** Shorts puts the channel name, title and subscribe button at the bottom, and its buttons on the right sit slightly higher than Instagram's. Keeping the captions centre-low, above the bottom ~20%, works on both *(inferred)*.
