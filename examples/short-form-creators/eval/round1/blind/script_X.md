# Reel: "Why your SIP isn't making money: 3 mistakes"

**Target length:** about 35 seconds, about 95 spoken words at a brisk talking pace.
**Format:** you talking to camera, vertical, phone at eye level, with a text card on screen for each beat.

---

## The hook (0:00–0:03)

**On screen (first frame, before you speak):**
Your own app screenshot (Groww, Zerodha Coin, Kuvera, whichever you use) or a mock-up of one, filling the top half of the frame:
> Invested: ₹2,40,000 · Current: ₹2,46,000 · **+2.5%**

Big text over it:
> **2 years of SIP. ₹6,000 profit?!**

**You say (looking straight into the lens, no "hi guys"):**
> "Two years of SIP and *this* is your profit? You're making one of these 3 mistakes."

(About 15 words, said fast. The screenshot carries the first second, so the hook lands even with the sound off.)

**Backup hooks if you want to test another one later:**
- Say: "Your SIP isn't broken. These 3 habits are." Text: **Your SIP isn't the problem.**
- Say: "If you did this in a market crash, you lost money twice." Text: **Mistake #1 costs the most.**

## Fact check before you post
- **Mistake 1 numbers:** ₹10,000 ÷ ₹50 NAV = 200 units, and ₹10,000 ÷ ₹40 NAV = 250 units. Correct.
- **Mistake 2 numbers:** ₹10k a month for 20 years comes to about ₹1 crore at 12% and about ₹87 lakh at 11%, a gap of roughly ₹12–13 lakh. The gap between direct and regular expense ratios is usually 0.5–1.5% for equity funds, so "around 1%" is fair.
- **Mistake 3:** SEBI's riskometer and most AMCs suggest a horizon of 5 years or more for equity funds. Don't promise any specific return.

## Filming checklist (today, on your phone)
1. **Light:** sit facing a window, not with it behind you. Shoot in 1080p at 30fps, using the back camera if you can frame yourself.
2. **Audio:** wear wired earphones with a mic, or film in a small room with soft furnishings. Bad audio loses more viewers than bad video.
3. **Shoot in chunks:** do one take per row of the table, so 5 short clips. It's easier than one perfect 35-second take, and you can jump-cut between them.
4. **Energy:** say it about 10% faster and louder than feels natural. On a phone screen it will come across as normal.
5. **Screenshot:** take a screenshot of your app (blur your folio number and name), or build a clean mock-up in Canva.
6. **Edit:** cut every breath and pause. Add auto-captions (most people watch on mute) and keep them in the middle of the screen, clear of the bottom 20%, which Instagram's UI covers. Put the music very low, or use none.
