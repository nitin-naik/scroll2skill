# Reel script: "Why your SIP isn't making money: 3 mistakes"

**Length:** about 38 s (95 spoken words at a calm ~2.5 words/sec)
**What viewers come away with:** they can tell whether one of these three mistakes is eating their SIP returns, and what to do about it.

## Script (film it as-is)

| Time | Spoken (exact words) | On screen (text / what we see) |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | **"SIP flat after two years? Three mistakes cause that."** | **First frame:** a phone held up to the camera showing a mutual fund app with returns near 0% (use a sample or blurred screen, not a fake "my portfolio"). Text: **SIP stuck at 0%?** |
| 3–7 s | "Most people blame the fund. It's one of these." | You, face to camera. Text: **3 mistakes** |
| 7–13 s | "One: judging equity after two years. Markets stay flat that long. Give it five plus." | Text: **1. Checking too early** → then **5+ years** |
| 13–16 s | "That one's common. The second one hurts more." | Quick cut / lean in. No text, or **2 →** |
| 16–21 s | "Two: pausing your SIP when markets fall. That's when you buy more units." | Text: **2. Stopping in a crash**. Optional: simple down-arrow graphic with "more units" |
| 21–24 s | "And the third one hides in your statement." | You tapping the phone / holding up a statement. Text: **3 → ?** |
| 24–33 s | "Three: regular plans. Around one percent extra in fees. On ten thousand a month for twenty years, that's over twelve lakh rupees." | Text: **3. Regular plan** → then **₹12 lakh+ lost** (show "₹10k/month · 20 yrs" in small text underneath) |
| 33–38 s | "Which one are you making? Ask below, I reply to every one." | Face to camera, pointing down. Text: **Ask me below** |

## Two alternative hooks

| Frame | Spoken | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| Number-first | "₹12 lakh. That's what one SIP mistake can cost you." (10 words) | **Check your plan** |
| Mistake-first | "Stopping your SIP when markets crash? That's one of three costly mistakes." (12 words) | **Don't hit pause** |

(The number-first hook works well with the same script; just move "three mistakes" into the lead line: "And it's one of three mistakes.")

## Caption options

1. "Your SIP isn't broken. These 3 mistakes are. Which one are you making? 👇 #SIP #mutualfunds #personalfinance"
2. "Stopped your SIP when Nifty fell? Read this before you do it again. #SIP #investing #moneytips"

## CTA note

I used the "ask in the comments, I reply to every one" CTA because you can deliver it yourself at 8k followers, and it's the format linked to the highest comment rate in the examples I drew on (one creator's video, so treat it as a promising lead, not a sure thing). If you already run Instagram DM automation (e.g. ManyChat), you could swap in: "Comment DIRECT and I'll send you how to check your plan." Only do that if you've tested the automation works on your account today.

## Check before filming

- **₹12 lakh figure:** I calculated it as ₹10,000/month for 20 years at 12% a year (≈ ₹99.9 lakh) vs 11% (≈ ₹87.4 lakh), a gap of ≈ ₹12.6 lakh. These are assumed returns for illustration, not promises. Put "assumed 12% vs 11% returns" in small text on screen or in the caption.
- **"Around one percent extra in fees":** the gap between regular and direct plans varies by fund, often roughly 0.5–1.5% for equity funds. Check 2–3 real funds' expense ratios (regular vs direct) on AMFI or the fund factsheet before you say it. If your examples come out lower, change the line to match.
- **"Markets stay flat that long":** true for Indian equity historically, but have one example ready in case commenters push back (look up a flat Nifty stretch you're confident about).
- **"Five plus years":** a common guideline for equity funds, not a guarantee. Fine to say, just don't call it a rule.
- **Compliance:** keep this general and educational. Don't name specific funds to buy, and add "Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks. Not investment advice." to the caption. If you're not SEBI-registered, double-check the current SEBI rules on finfluencers before posting.
- **Assumptions about you:** I didn't use a personal story because you didn't give me one. If you actually made one of these mistakes yourself, opening with that ("I paused my SIP in 2020. Here's what it cost me.") is likely to work better than any hook above. Only use it if it's true.
- **First frame:** don't show a real portfolio that isn't yours, and don't present a mocked-up screen as your own results.
