# Friction: short-form-script skill

1. **"Write the hook (0–3 s)" vs "Spoken hook ≤ 12 words"** combined with step 6's "about 2.5 spoken words per second": 12 words at 2.5 w/s is 4.8 s, so the two rules conflict. My first hook (11 words) ran 4.4 s. Guess: the time limit wins, so keep the spoken hook to about 7–8 words. I cut it to 8.

2. **"Hook → Lead (carry them to ~10 s) → Point 1 → open loop → Point 2 → open loop → Point 3 → CTA"** with "A 30–40 s reel holds 3 points at most": a 3-point reel with a lead to ~10 s and a CTA leaves about 6 s per point, which doesn't fit realistic Indian-finance points at 2.5 w/s. Guess: shortened the lead to ~4 s (ending at 7 s, not 10 s) and kept each point to one or two short sentences.

3. **"Pick a frame. Prefer 'here's what happened when I did it'... never invent a personal story for them."** The user's topic is already a "what to do/avoid" list and they gave no personal result. The skill says to fall back on "a specific case or a number they can stand behind", but doesn't say how to do that inside a listicle topic. Guess: used a computed number (₹12 lakh) as the proof element and suggested a true personal opener as an optional upgrade.

4. **"First frame already shows something: the result, a number..."**: there's no guidance on what to show when the creator has no real result to put on screen. Guess: a sample or blurred app screen, plus a warning not to present it as their own portfolio.

5. **"On-screen hook text = 2–4 words"** applies only to the hook. Nothing covers text length for the body beats. Guess: kept body text to 2–5 words per beat.

6. **"Comment-keyword CTA ... when the user can deliver it ... status UNVERIFIED for 2026, so check before promising"**: it isn't clear whether I should pick this CTA for an Instagram user (the skill's own Instagram example) or default to the reply-promise. Guess: reply-promise as the main CTA, since an 8k account can deliver it without tooling, and keyword CTA as an option.

7. **"Output ... 1. Script table, 2. Hook variants, 3. Titles/captions"** vs **"for YouTube Shorts, prefer the 'I did X' framing"**: there's no guidance for Instagram captions (length, hashtags, disclaimers). Guess: two short captions with a few hashtags.

8. **"Fact-check every number and claim ... flag anything the user must verify (rates, returns, regulations)"**: it doesn't say whether to compute illustrative numbers myself or which assumptions to use (e.g. return rates). Guess: computed the SIP future value at 12% vs 11% and disclosed the assumptions. The expense-ratio gap (0.5–1.5%) comes from my general knowledge, so I flagged it as "verify".

9. **Missing: regulatory/compliance guidance for finance content** (SEBI finfluencer rules, the mandatory mutual-fund risk disclaimer). The skill only says "regulations" in passing. Guess: I added a compliance check from my own knowledge, without verifying the current SEBI rules.

10. **"Hook variants: ... each with a different frame (result-first, number-first or mistake-first)"**: it's unclear whether the main hook counts as one of the frames (so the variants must use the other two) and what "result-first" means when there's no result. Guess: treated the main hook as result-first (a flat SIP screen) and wrote number-first and mistake-first variants.

11. **"say when you do" (use own judgment)**: no format for marking where I used my own judgment. Guess: explained inline in a "CTA note" and in "Check before filming".
